# Supporting MakerOps Labs

MakerOps Labs is a free, self-hosted toolkit built for small makers and manufacturers. It took real time and money to create, and it takes ongoing effort to maintain, document, and improve. The tools are used in the author's own business — which means they stay practical — but keeping them free and available for everyone else is a deliberate choice that depends on the community finding them worth supporting.

If MakerOps Labs saves you a monthly subscription, solves a real problem in your operation, or just points you in a useful direction, consider supporting it.

## What Funding Goes Toward

- **Maintainer time** — design, development, debugging, and review
- **Documentation** — writing and hosting guides that make setup actually work
- **Infrastructure** — test environments, demo instances, and tooling
- **Hardware** — testing on the low-cost hardware the project targets

No funding goes toward investors or growth metrics. This is a tool built to be used, not a product built to be sold.

---

## Non-Monetary Support

Financial contributions aren't the only way to help sustain this project:

- **Star the repositories** — visibility helps others find the tools
- **Share it** — mention it in maker forums, Discord servers, subreddits, or anywhere your community hangs out
- **File bug reports** — a well-described issue is worth hours of blind debugging
- **Open PRs** — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how
- **Tell us how you're using it** — knowing what's working in real operations helps prioritize what to build next

Thank you for being part of this.
